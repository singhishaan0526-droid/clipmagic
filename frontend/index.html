Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Clip Magic's architecture, keyframing, and export engine.

Does Clip Magic run completely in the browser? +
Yes! Clip Magic uses modern WebAssembly (WASM), Web Audio API, and high-performance HTML5 Canvas rendering. Your video files stay entirely on your device for unmatched privacy and speed.
How does the Lossless Stream Copy export work? +
When you perform straight cuts, trims, and splits without visual effects, Clip Magic utilizes FFmpeg `-c copy` stream copying. It bypasses the re-encoding pass entirely, completing your export in less than 2 seconds with zero quality loss.
Can I customize keyframes after applying a Quick Animate preset? +
Absolutely! Applying any 1-tap preset generates keyframe nodes in the shared unified animation schema. You can click "Convert to Custom Keyframes" at any time to fine-tune individual diamond markers, easing curves, and position coordinates.
Can I export directly to TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts? +
Yes, Clip Magic includes built-in platform presets (9:16 Vertical, 16:9 Landscape, 1:1 Square, and 4:5 Portrait) with automatic reframing.
