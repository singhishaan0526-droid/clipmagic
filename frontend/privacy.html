Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 14, 2026 • Effective Date: October 14, 2026
Core Privacy Architecture: Clip Magic is designed with a client-first privacy model. Your uploaded video footage, trimming, multi-track timeline editing, WebGL shader color grading, and FFmpeg exports execute locally inside your web browser. Raw video files are not permanently stored on our servers.

1. Information We Collect and Process

Depending on how you interact with Clip Magic, we collect and process the following categories of data:

  • Local Project Data: Timeline clips, cut markers, audio sync offsets, speed ramps, keyframe nodes, and filter presets. This data is stored strictly in your browser's localStorage and IndexedDB.
  • Audio Snippets for AI Transcription: When you voluntarily activate automated lyric transcription, audio speech recognition, or subtitle synchronization, extracted audio segments are temporarily transmitted through our secure API to Google Gemini AI services for transcription processing.
  • Technical and Device Data: IP address, browser user-agent, operating system version, and device performance metrics used strictly for rate limiting, DDoS mitigation, and server security.
  • Cookie and Consent Preferences: Anonymous storage of your cookie consent preferences (accepted, rejected, or customized categories).

2. How Uploaded Media is Processed

We believe in maximum transparency regarding how your media files are handled:

  • In-Browser Video Processing: Video playback, canvas overlay compositing, and rendering use HTML5 Video, WebGL GLSL shaders, and WebAssembly FFmpeg. These operations occur directly on your computer's CPU and GPU.
  • No Cloud Storage of Raw Videos: Clip Magic does not copy, upload, or archive your source video files to central cloud file storage. When you close or clear your local browser project, your local browser memory is released.
  • AI Subtitle Processing: If you use the AI Transcription feature, only the audio track needed for transcription is submitted to the Gemini API endpoint. This transmission is encrypted with TLS 1.3. Google does not use data submitted via our enterprise API endpoints to train foundational AI models.

3. Third-Party Services and Sub-processors

Clip Magic integrates with a carefully vetted set of third-party infrastructure providers:

  • Google Gemini AI (Google Cloud): Utilized solely for speech-to-text transcript generation and lyric alignment.
  • Content Delivery Network & Hosting: Edge hosting and static asset delivery provided through Vercel and secure edge nodes.
  • Google Fonts: Static typography assets loaded via Google Fonts CDN for optimized display.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

Clip Magic uses local storage and cookies to maintain essential web application functions:

  • Essential Storage: Required for storing your timeline projects, theme preferences, and preventing data loss when switching tools. These cannot be disabled as the video editor cannot function without them.
  • Cookie Consent Banner: We provide an interactive cookie management banner allowing you to accept or reject non-essential analytics tracking at any time. You can change your choice via the Cookie Preferences link in the footer.

5. Data Retention and Security

Because Clip Magic processes video client-side, retention is under your control:

  • Project timelines and clip references persist in your browser until you explicitly click "Delete Project" in your Creator Dashboard or clear your browser's site data.
  • Server-side request logs (IP address, timestamps) used for API rate limiting are retained for no more than 30 days and automatically rotated.
  • All server communications enforce HTTPS with Strict-Transport-Security (HSTS) and modern TLS protocols.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including GDPR, CCPA, and UK DPA), you have the right to:

  • Access, download, or export your projects at any time using our "Export Project Backup (.json)" feature.
  • Erase your data by deleting local projects or clearing browser IndexedDB storage.
  • Withdraw cookie consent by clicking "Cookie Preferences" in the website footer.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions, feedback, or inquiries regarding this Privacy Policy or our security architecture, please contact our team at privacy@clipmagic.ai.
